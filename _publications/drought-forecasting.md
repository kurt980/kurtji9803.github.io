This collaborative study develops a joint wavelet decomposition approach for drought forecasting, using historical windows to prevent future-information leakage. It combines local and large-scale climate predictors and evaluates forecasts across temporal scales.

I contributed to this research through my environmental-statistics work at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign and am the second author.

**Authors:** E. Vivas, Chengyan Ji, and Lelys Bravo De Guenni.
