This conference paper investigates brain connectivity and ADHD using multimodal neuroimaging, genetic data, and Mendelian randomization. It is part of my research at the University of Virginia on statistical genetics and adolescent mental health.

I am the first author and presented this work at SPIE Medical Imaging in San Diego in February 2025.

**Authors:** Chengyan Ji, Seonjoo Lee, Stefanie Sequeira, Jin Jin, and Aiying Zhang.

**Publication:** *Proceedings of SPIE*, volume 13411, *Medical Imaging 2025: Imaging Informatics*, article 134110V (2025).
