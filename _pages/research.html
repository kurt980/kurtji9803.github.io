Bayesian spatial extremes

George Mason University · February 2026–present

I work on Bayesian models with nonstationary generalized extreme-value margins and spatial dependence. My contributions include simulation design, model comparisons, posterior diagnostics, numerical investigation, and reproducible R/Slurm workflows.

The current project compares joint and two-stage estimation across 800 simulated datasets. Joint estimation improves empirical interval coverage and fitted-site spatial-field accuracy. Coefficient freezing and residual undercoverage remain open; held-out prediction and real-data evaluation are ongoing.

Neuroimaging & statistical genetics

University of Virginia · 2024–2025

Studied brain connectivity and adolescent mental health using neuroimaging and genetic data, linear mixed models, and Mendelian randomization. My work included data processing, quality control, sensitivity analysis, and interpretation of causal assumptions.

Environmental forecasting

University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign · 2023–2024

Contributed to drought forecasting with climate predictors, autoregressive models, SVMs, LSTMs, and wavelet decomposition. Evaluation used historical windows to avoid future-information leakage.
